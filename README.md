# Quantitative Finance & Data Analytics Portfolio

## Core Technical Stack

* **Languages & Environments:** Python (NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib), SQL (PostgreSQL), SAS, Advanced Excel.
* **Financial Engineering:** Stochastic Calculus, Modern Portfolio Theory, Derivative Hedging, Time Value of Money (TVM), Asset Retirement Obligations (ARO).
* **Econometrics & Statistics:** OLS, Fixed Effects, 2SLS Instrumental Variables, Heteroscedasticity Diagnostics, Stationarity Analysis.

## Featured Engineering & Research Projects

* **[EN] [Econometric Research: Heroic Networks & Collaboration](./Econometrics%20Project/)**
Applied advanced econometrics in **SAS** to analyze a comprehensive 34,942-row dataset. Deployed OLS, Within-Transformation Fixed Effects, and 2SLS Instrumental Variables, utilizing White-robust standard errors (HCCME) to isolate causal effects and mitigate statistical biases.
* **[EN] [Quantitative Option Pricing: BSM vs. CRR](./Option%20Pricing%20BSM%20vs%20CRR/)**
Engineered an Object-Oriented **Python** pricing engine for AAPL derivatives. Extracted Implied Volatility via Newton-Raphson, visualized Delta-hedging dynamics, and empirically tested Geometric Brownian Motion assumptions against market leptokurtosis (fat tails).
* **[Strategic Financial Analysis & Quantitative Modeling](./Strategic%20Financial%20Analysis/)**
Architected dynamic, multi-layered **Excel** models for institutional decision-making. Executed Variance-Covariance matrix operations for portfolio optimization, engineered multi-period binomial trees, and stress-tested a €27.7B Asset Retirement Obligation (ARO).
* **[Relational Database Architecture: Art Gallery](./Projet%20SQL/)**
Designed and deployed a robust **PostgreSQL** database ensuring strict referential integrity. Leveraged advanced DQL (multi-joins, subquery exclusions, aggregations) to extract commercial intelligence and optimize inventory liquidity.
* **[Macroeconomic Strategy: China vs. Japan FX Policies](./Finance%20Internationale/)**
Analyzed the systemic, real-world impacts of diverging monetary regimes (PBOC's managed float vs. BoJ's Abenomics), mapping their direct influence on international competitiveness, imported inflation, and global trade balances.
* **[Interactive Financial Calculator](./Calculatrice%20financi%C3%A8re%20Python/)**
Built a modular, highly vectorized **Python** (NumPy/Pandas) CLI application. Automated the generation of debt amortization schedules and dynamically mapped the Markowitz Efficient Frontier to calculate the exact Minimum Variance Portfolio (MVP).
